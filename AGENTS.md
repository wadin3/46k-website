# 46K website context

For brand, design or editorial work, start at `docs/brand/README.md` and
read `docs/brand/v2.0/46K_00_START_HERE_v2.0.md`. Use the relevant written
specification and select only the reference boards relevant to the task.
Current user instructions take precedence over the supplied documents.

Official production logos are the four unchanged PNGs in
`public/brand/logos/`. Never redraw, retype, recolor or regenerate them.
Design reference boards remain internal under `docs/brand/v2.0/references/`;
their incidental copy, data, colors and logo renderings are not authoritative.

Scientific content must distinguish established evidence, emerging evidence
and 46K interpretation. Prepare new articles as drafts for review.
