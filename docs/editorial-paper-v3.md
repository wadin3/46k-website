# Editorial paper revision

Review-only implementation of the owner's Forsta.docx and paper prototype.
The supplied prototype is design inspiration, not an approved article or
a source for scientific claims. Its external photographs are not copied.
Existing article Markdown, evidence notes, sources, credits, draft flags,
translation keys, category definitions and CMS schema remain unchanged.

Explore now has a Pitch hero with the requested Swedish heading and deck
and their English equivalent, and magazine-cover links. The filter uses
client-side visibility changes and History API navigation, not page loads.
A measured full-list footprint avoids scroll clamping when a category has
fewer articles. URL categories, history, empty state, translation links,
keyboard access and reduced-motion behavior remain supported.

PaperSheet is a numbered surface wrapper. Article sections are grouped at
text boundaries, retaining their original order and anchor IDs. Cover,
reading spreads and sources get sequential folios. Desktop uses no more
than two reading columns, mobile one. Sheets grow with their contents;
there is no fixed-height clipping or duplicated copy for pagination.

The neutral surround and three light, cool off-white grounds create depth.
Sparsely varied ink, registration and fold-memory treatments stay inside
the outer margins, away from copy and photography. No repeated wallpaper,
yellow/sepia staining, grunge, large folds or decorative spots. The only
bitmap overlay reuses the existing approved internal production print
asset at very low opacity, clipped to a margin strip. Photography is
natural or grayscale, with existing illustrative-image disclosures.

No new runtime or animation dependencies, generated assets or media
hotlinks. Shared header/footer, Mission, Assessment and Contact unchanged.
