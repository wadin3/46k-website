## 1. BRAND CORE

**46K**  
**DECODING FOOTBALL INTELLIGENCE**

Founding ambition: **SWEDEN. WORLD CHAMPIONS. 2046.**  
2046 is not a prediction. It is a direction.

Simple explanation: **We use cognitive science to understand and develop
football intelligence.**

Operating model: **UNDERSTAND → ASSESS → DEVELOP**

Core belief: **Talent is more than what you can see today.**

Brand stance: **Football first. Science underneath. Bold in ambition.**

46K should feel like an intelligent outsider in football:
evidence-driven, sharp, contemporary, development-oriented, slightly
irreverent and willing to challenge assumptions.

Internal archetype: **Moneyball intelligence in a Filippa K blazer and
worn Vans.**

## 2. PERSONALITY BOUNDARIES

Always aim for:

- High performance, not macho.
- Scientific, not academic.
- Premium, not luxury.
- Edgy, not aggressive.
- Playful, never childish.
- Simple, not dumbed-down.
- Ambitious, not grandiose.
- Football-native, not football cliché.

Do not drift into:

- generic sporttech
- generic AI/SaaS
- neuroscience hype
- corporate consultancy
- motivational coaching
- gaming/esports
- black-and-gold luxury
- tactics-board identity

## 3. EVIDENCE DISCIPLINE

Internally classify substantive claims:

**WE KNOW** — established general psychological/cognitive knowledge.  
**EVIDENCE SUGGESTS** — promising/incomplete football-specific evidence
or findings with important limitations.  
**WE THINK** — 46K interpretation, hypothesis, extrapolation or
recommendation.

Never:

- turn correlation into causation
- turn group findings into certain individual predictions
- present promising evidence as settled science
- claim a test measures exact true football intelligence
- claim a test determines future professional success
- imply that 46K replaces coaches, scouts, observation or match data

Brand rule: **Be bold about the question; be accurate about what the
evidence can support.**

## 4. VOICE

**Complex science. Simple language.**

Write:

- concise
- clear
- informed
- confident
- slightly dry
- football-aware
- low-jargon
- willing to make a point

Do not sound:

- academic unless the format requires it
- corporate
- like a generic startup
- like a motivational speaker
- like an adult trying to imitate teenagers

Dry, intelligent, sparse humor is allowed.

Staccato is emphasis, not normal speech.

## 5. THE THREE GEARS

**THE GEAR FOLLOWS THE CONTENT — NOT THE CHANNEL.**

### EDITORIAL — ideas, insights, perspectives

Use for research, essays, 2046, larger questions and storytelling.

Visual tendencies:

- Paper-dominant
- light/faded grayscale football photography
- restrained Gold
- sober serif
- subtle print/paper materiality
- short Gold line

Feeling: calm, intelligent, spacious, human.

### CORE — tests, data, development

Use for assessment, player profiles, product, data, feedback,
recommendations and development.

Visual tendencies:

- Paper / Pitch / Graphite balance
- simple test-inspired stimuli
- dots, lines, profiles, radar/spider charts
- portraits with information overlays
- coaching, feedback, cones, bibs
- mobile assessment
- clear path from measurement to development

Typography: **thin/light uppercase, normal proportions. NOT condensed.**

Feeling: precise, contemporary, useful.

### PERFORMANCE — training, match, results

Use for training, competition, perception, decisions, player-facing
application and performance.

Visual tendencies:

- Graphite/Pitch dominant
- movement
- selective blur
- perception arcs
- angles
- restrained arrows
- fractions of seconds
- training details
- bold/black uppercase, optionally slanted

Feeling: faster, darker, competitive, intelligent.

Never macho, superhero-like or gaming-HUD heavy.

## 6. LOCKED COLORS

- **46K Paper:** `#F5F2E9`
- **46K Graphite:** `#383D3E`
- **46K Pitch:** `#285C43`
- **46K Gold:** `#C29D45`

Rule: **Paper, Graphite and Pitch build the world. Gold points to what
matters.**

Gold is a marker, not wallpaper.

Paper may carry subtle fibre/print grain.  
Gold may carry restrained brushed/worn texture.  
In the production logo, the Gold K **always** carries the approved
worn-Gold surface; a flat Gold K is a technical fallback only.  
Never use shiny casino/Rolex/champagne Gold.

## 7. TYPOGRAPHY ROLES

**Core:** light and precise. Thin/light uppercase. Normal proportions.
Never interpret thin as narrow/condensed.

**Performance:** heavy and moving. Bold/Black uppercase. May be somewhat
compact and slanted/italic.

**Editorial:** quiet and considered. Sober serif, normal capitalization,
moderate weight, generous space.

**Functional:** modern neutral sans-serif for body text, navigation, UI,
labels, charts and test interfaces.

Exact font families are not locked unless a task supplies approved
fonts/assets.

## 9. GRAPHIC LANGUAGE

Primary rule: **Visualize information — not just football.**

Secondary rule: **Visualize thinking through football.**

Development rule: **Measurement should lead toward development.**

Useful recurring elements:

- datapoints
- lines
- dots
- scales
- profiles
- radar/spider charts
- simple test stimuli
- perception arcs
- attention markers
- angles
- restrained arrows
- timing/fractions of seconds
- selective focus
- simple pitch zones when they communicate information/decisions
- short Gold line

Avoid decorative tech.

Do not use glowing brains, neural-network heads, generic circuitry,
tactical-arrow clutter or gaming HUD.

## 10. PHOTOGRAPHY

Default to older teenagers, young adults and adults — not children.

Show football players:

- thinking
- perceiving
- deciding
- learning
- receiving feedback
- training
- developing

Training is at least as important as match imagery.

Editorial: light/faded grayscale, restrained football romance.  
Core: calm portraits, mobile assessment, video analysis, coaching,
feedback, development.  
Performance: darker movement, training/match, perception and timing.

Avoid stock-photo team huddles, exaggerated celebration, warrior
imagery, flames, lightning, neon and superstar worship.

## 11. UI AND DATA

Use airy layouts, disciplined grids, strong hierarchy and generous
negative space.

UI should be understated and functional — not generic SaaS, gaming,
crypto, medical software or corporate HR.

Data must be clear before decorative.

Player-facing product flow should tend toward:

**RESULT → INSIGHT → RECOMMENDATION → TRAINING → FOLLOW-UP**

Assessment must not terminate at a score.

## 12. CONTENT

Primary pillars:

- UNDERSTAND
- ASSESS
- DEVELOP
- 2046

Useful logic:

**HOOK → INSIGHT → EVIDENCE → 46K TAKE → APPLICATION**

Desired content effects:

- Teach me something.
- Make me question something.
- Make me want to know something about myself or my players.

## 13. AUTOMATIC REJECTION

Revise an output if it contains any of these without explicit
instruction:

- glowing brain
- neural-network head
- generic AI circuitry
- neon green
- shiny metallic Gold
- black-and-gold luxury
- child players as default
- macho slogans
- warrior/domination aesthetics
- generic stock team huddle
- tactics board as identity
- dense tactical arrows
- gaming HUD
- generic SaaS dashboard
- condensed Core typography
- ornate/cut/folded K
- different logo by gear
- invented separate icon
- pseudo-scientific decorative data
- unsupported certainty
- deterministic talent prediction
- assessment with no development relevance

## 14. FINAL CHECK

Before delivery, verify:

1.  Does football come first?
2.  Is there real thinking/information underneath?
3.  Is the evidence calibrated correctly?
4.  Is one gear clearly dominant?
5.  Is Gold pointing to something meaningful?
6.  Is development visible when relevant?
7.  Are the logo and typography rules respected?
8.  Does the work avoid macho, academic, luxury, gaming and startup
    clichés?
9.  Does it make Football Intelligence feel worth understanding,
    assessing and developing?

If any answer is no, revise before delivery.
