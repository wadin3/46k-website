**SWEDEN. WORLD CHAMPIONS. 2046.**

It is a deliberately ambitious question.

Not because we can forecast the winner of a tournament twenty years away. We cannot.

But an ambition this far away changes the questions we ask today.

## What might football be missing?

Some players develop earlier. Some find the right environment. Some get noticed at the right moment. Many things influence a football pathway.

We want to investigate one part of this bigger picture: how might a deeper understanding of cognitive capabilities improve the ways we identify and develop potential?

That does not replace coaches, football environments or the game itself.

It adds another question to the conversation.

**WE THINK:** If football learns to see more dimensions of player potential, it may also discover more ways to support player development.

2046 is not a prediction. It is a direction.
